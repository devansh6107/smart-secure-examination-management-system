Help & Frequently Asked Questions

Guidance for Students, Invigilators, and Administrators using ExamGuard AI

Contact Support Desk

ExamGuard AI uses role-based authentication with secure password verification (PBKDF2-SHA256). Each user belongs to one of three roles:

  • Admin: System administration, room scheduling, and master reports.
  • Student: Viewing registered exams, seating allocations, and generating verified hall tickets.
  • Invigilator: Monitoring exam rooms, marking attendance, and reviewing AI proctoring alerts.

For demo purposes, the login screen provides one-click account buttons for sample profiles in each role.

System Administrators hold full administrative authority over master data and exam workflows:

  • Maintain departments, subjects, classrooms, and seat grids.
  • Onboard and manage student profiles and invigilator faculty assignments.
  • Schedule examinations, set dates, start/end timings, and exam types (CAT1, CAT2, FAT).
  • Execute automated seat allocations and review capacity limits.
  • Supervise all flagged AI proctoring alerts and recorded examination incidents.
  • Generate and print consolidated attendance reports and exam summary analytics.

Once logged into the Student Portal:

  1. Navigate to My Exams on the dashboard.
  2. Locate the relevant exam in your schedule table.
  3. Click the Hall Ticket button in the action column.
  4. Review your allocated building, room number, assigned seat identifier, and exam instructions.
  5. Use the built-in Print Ticket action to obtain a clean physical copy formatted with our print styles.

Exam registrations link enrolled students with scheduled examinations:

  • Administrators manage course registrations via the Exams → Registrations module.
  • The database enforces strict uniqueness constraints: a student cannot be registered twice for the same exam.
  • If an administrative cancellation is required, administrators can cancel a registration with confirmation feedback.

ExamGuard AI features automated deterministic seat allocation algorithms:

  • Rooms have physical grid layouts (Rows A–Z, Columns 1–N) generated according to room dimensions.
  • The allocation engine validates room capacity against registered student count before allocation.
  • If registered students exceed available unallocated seats, a Capacity Overflow safeguard prevents partial corruption.
  • Students receive sequential, unique seat numbers preventing collisions.

During an active examination session:

  1. The assigned Invigilator opens the Exam Operations Sheet for their hall.
  2. For each seated student, the invigilator verifies candidate identity via physical ID or simulated Biometric/Face check.
  3. The invigilator marks candidate status as Present, Absent, or Malpractice.
  4. Timestamps and the verifying invigilator's faculty ID are recorded into the audit trail.

In this academic DBMS implementation, the AI Proctoring engine operates as a heuristic event simulation and surveillance management pipeline:

  • Flags patterns such as Mobile Phone Detected, Multiple Persons Detected, Looking Away, and Suspicious Movement.
  • Each alert includes a confidence score (e.g., 75%–98%) and the exact timestamp of detection.
  • Invigilators and Administrators can review pending alerts, inspect student seat details, and mark them as Reviewed or Dismissed.
  • Note: The current implementation utilizes algorithmic simulation data rather than proprietary continuous video cloud pipelines.

When an invigilator observes an examination rule violation or technical emergency:

  1. Navigate to Invigilator Panel → Incidents.
  2. Fill out the Incident Log form specifying the exam, candidate registration number, incident type (e.g. Unauthorized Material, Misconduct, Tech Failure), and severity (Low, Medium, High, Critical).
  3. Submit the record. The incident is permanently logged and immediately elevated to the Admin incident overview.

If you experience difficulty signing into ExamGuard AI:

  • Verify credentials: Ensure your registration number or faculty username is spelled correctly. Passwords are case-sensitive.
  • Use Demo Profiles: If testing the prototype, use the quick-fill demo buttons located on the login card.
  • Account Deactivation: If your account has been placed into Inactive status by an administrator, contact the Examination Cell.
  • Session Issues: Ensure browser cookies and local storage are enabled for this site.
